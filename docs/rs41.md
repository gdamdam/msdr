# RS41 radiosonde decoder

msdr decodes Vaisala RS41 radiosonde telemetry in-process, for anyone tracking sondes. It takes signed interleaved int16 complex IQ at a sample rate of at least 57.6 ksample/s.

## How it works

1. Acquire the 200-bit alternating preamble across the twelve integer sample phases at the 57.6 ksample/s working rate.
2. Find the frame sync word.
3. Descramble the standard or extended frame.
4. Repair its two interleaved shortened RS(255,231) codewords, up to 12 symbol errors each.
5. Emit a frame only when the RS41 sub-block CRC checks pass after correction.

## Reed–Solomon correction

- This is a clean-room implementation: GF(256), primitive polynomial `0x11d`, roots α⁰ through α²³.
- Shortening and interleave follow the public [RS41 frame description](https://github.com/rs1729/RS/blob/master/rs41/rs41.txt). No third-party decoder is copied.
- Correction is reported as `rs_corrected_symbols` and in the integrity note.

## Tests and limits

- The regression suite covers the published generator coefficients, clean frames, 12 corrupted symbols in each lane, rejection beyond that limit, sample-phase offsets, feed chunk boundaries, and a synthetic SigMF replay through `ReplayDecodeSession`.
- The fixtures are generated test signals and do not establish off-air performance.
- The timing search covers integer sample phases only. It does not estimate a continuously varying clock.
